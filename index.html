Why Semantic HTML Matters

By HTML Basics Team - October 11, 2026

Semantic HTML gives content meaning. It helps browsers, assistive technologies, and search engines understand how a page is organized.

The benefits of semantic HTML

Elements such as main, nav, and article describe the purpose of their content instead of just how it looks.

Assistive technology can navigate page landmarks.
Search engines can better interpret the page structure.
Other developers can understand and maintain the markup.

Choose elements by meaning

Use a heading for a heading, a button for an action, and a link for navigation.
